# Changelog

All notable changes to the World Gump Licence are recorded here. The version
in [`VERSION`](VERSION) is the current text.

## 1.0 — 2026-10-26

- Initial public publication of the World Gump Licence.
- Signed by the Open Gump Foundation. Signing key fingerprint:
  `680C 808F 7B47 1985 E78E F452 C980 D1A1 41D9 EE67`.
- Covers the open Gump stack: GAUTH, GAI, the Gumpworks content system, and
  projects that build on them.
- Grant of rights, distribution conditions, patent grant, trademark
  reservation, contributions, warranty disclaimer, liability limitation,
  termination with a 30-day cure period, and acceptance.
